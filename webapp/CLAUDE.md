@AGENTS.md

## Pages

- `/` — Musical Box: ring connection, session recording, song generation. Each person has a fixed ring, passed to `RingCard` as `ringName`: Person 1 wears size 9 `R02_AF03`, Person 2 size 11 `R02_D7B0`. Its Connect button names that ring and Chrome's chooser lists only it ("Choose a different ring" lists any). Chrome remembers a chosen ring across reloads only with `chrome://flags/#enable-web-bluetooth-new-permissions-backend` and `#enable-experimental-web-platform-features`; then Connect finds it by name without the chooser.
- `/syncwave` — full-screen heartbeat waves for a TV, opened in a tab by the SyncWave button on `/`: one wave per ring mirrored around a shared axis, each a range of ridges with solid flanks, both always travelling right, each BPM shown as a figure among the ridges (`components/waveform.tsx`). It follows the light/dark/cosmic colour mode, with its own switch that appears on mouse movement. Old `/syncwave/v1`–`v3` links redirect here (`next.config.ts`). It has no rings of its own: `/` broadcasts each ring's latest heart rate over a `BroadcastChannel` (`lib/heart-rate-channel.ts`), so both pages must be open in the same browser. Equal BPMs flash on the axis and tug the waves together every 2.4 s. The broadcast also carries the session's wait (`plan`: start time and expected ms, from `WINDOW_SEC`/`MUSIC_AT_SEC`/`SONG_EXPECTED_SEC` in `app/page.tsx`), shown as a bar under the waves on the `placebo()` curve in `lib/progress.ts` (80% at the expected time, then easing towards 99%; the music bar on `/` uses it too), and `song`: when the song starts playing on `/` (the player's `onStarted`, or 8 s after it is shown if it cannot) the waves morph into a big note with "Your song is ready!", and New Session turns them back. Both pages credit dimosthenisgkontolias.com. Quiet waiting music (`public/waiting-music.mp3`, a Suno take; `components/waiting-music.tsx`) plays on `/syncwave` from the first click on that tab (the browser allows sound only then; until then it says "Click anywhere to turn on the music"), carries on through Start, fades out the moment the song starts on `/`, starts again from the top with each New Session (`round` in the broadcast) and fades out and in again at the end of the track. Its volume is set by the slider under Now Playing on `/` (`musicVolume` in the broadcast, kept in localStorage via `lib/waiting-music.ts`). `?mock` replays a recorded session from the database, on the live site too; `?mock=<session id>` picks one; add `&song` to see the bar and the song arrive 15 s in.
